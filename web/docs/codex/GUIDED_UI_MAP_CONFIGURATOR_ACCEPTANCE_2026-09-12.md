2. From **Needs attention**, open an Email-backed Enquiry. Confirm its sender, subject, date and overview describe that exact Email; open the original; open/download the genuine document and image; confirm signature artwork is not offered as a business attachment.
3. At 100% and increased browser zoom, reach all existing/new Client and Project controls without a trapped inner scroll. Complete one disposable connection and review the file destination, duplicate/revision decision, exact outcome and next action. Retry one recoverable failure and confirm values are preserved.
4. Open a manual Enquiry and a missing-source Enquiry. Confirm neither invents Email content and both clearly explain what is available.
5. Check Enquiries, Clients, Projects, Files and Estimates. Confirm no generic **Quick actions** panel remains and that the needed action still exists in **Create**, the workspace header or record context.
6. Open **Project Map**. Check All stages and each stage filter, **Show all locations**, a shared-location count, each record in its popup, the unresolved-location action and exact **Open** navigation. Repeat in current Light/Dark and Legacy/V2 Light/Dark appearances.
7. Open an Estimate Position in the B92 Configurator. Switch current and Legacy/V2 Light/Dark appearances without changing the Position.
